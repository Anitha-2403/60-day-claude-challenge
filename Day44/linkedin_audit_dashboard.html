Headline

"B.Tech 3rd Year Student | Web Development & Data Analytics Enthusiast"
Problem

"Enthusiast" and "Student" are the only real signals here. No proof, no specialization, no reason to click further.

Why

Recruiters search by keyword. "Enthusiast" reads as hobbyist, not employable — and it buries your GenAI/Agentic AI experience entirely.

Cost

You don't surface in searches for "Generative AI," "Agentic AI," or "Python" — the exact terms recruiters type. Invisible before a human ever sees you.

About — First 2 Lines

2 / 10
"I am a B.Tech AIML student interested in AI technologies, Data Analytics, and Web Development."
Problem

This is the ONLY text visible before "see more" — the most valuable real estate on your profile — and it says "interested in," not "I do" or "I build."

Why

"Interested in" is passive mood, not capability. This line decides whether the rest gets read at all.

Cost

Most viewers never click "see more." If this line doesn't hook them, your real internship experience is never seen.

Experience

"Participating in an in-house GenAI internship covering Python, SQL, Machine Learning..."
Problem

The underlying content is genuinely strong — but every bullet opens with a passive verb: "Participating," "Completing," "Learning."

Why

Recruiters scan for action + result. "Learning AI workflows" reads student. "Applied prompt engineering to build X" reads contributor.

Cost

You're sitting on your most relevant experience — a real GenAI/Agentic AI internship — and writing it like a course description. The biggest gap on the whole profile.
