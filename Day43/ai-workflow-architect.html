    Workflow Summary

    You'll go from a blank repo to a live, indexed portfolio at a custom domain, with a working contact flow and a system for adding new projects in minutes.

    What "done" looks like

    • Live site on a custom domain, Lighthouse 90+ across the board
    • 3–6 case-study project pages with real detail, not just screenshots
    • Working contact form with spam protection and email delivery
    • Analytics + search console verified and collecting data
    • Content update takes < 10 minutes via CMS or MDX file

    Scope traps to avoid

    • Building a custom CMS admin panel instead of using MDX/headless CMS
    • Over-animating the homepage before any case studies exist
    • Picking a novel stack to "learn" instead of shipping — do that separately
    • Writing project copy last, under deadline pressure
