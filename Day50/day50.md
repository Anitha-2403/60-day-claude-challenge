# Day 50 – Defend Your Experience

## 📌 Challenge Overview

Today, I completed **Day 50** of the **#60DayClaudeChallenge** by building **Defend Your Experience**, an AI-powered interview preparation application using **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Claude AI**.

The application analyzes a user's resume or portfolio, extracts meaningful claims, and conducts an adaptive interview by asking personalized follow-up questions. It helps users build confidence by defending their experience with clear explanations and generates a detailed Defense Report highlighting strengths and areas for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project was to create an intelligent interview preparation platform that enables users to confidently explain and defend the experiences, projects, and skills mentioned in their resume during technical and HR interviews.
